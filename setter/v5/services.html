How it works

How we work together.

Every engagement runs the same way, from a 30-minute review to an engine that runs every day.

  1. 01

    Review

    A GTM systems review: your stack, your data, your funnel. We agree on the outcomes we'll own.

  2. 02

    Map

    We map your market, model your ideal customer and wire the signals that matter into your CRM.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build the plays inside your stack, test them and switch them on one at a time. The first is live in about 6 days.

  4. 04

    Run

    Agents run it every day. Each week your engineer shows what worked, fixes what didn't and ships the next play.
