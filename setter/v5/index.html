FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is GTM engineering?

The systems work behind revenue: the data, signals and automation in your CRM that put every rep, campaign and play on the right accounts at the right time.

How fast is the first play live?

About 6 days. We run the systems review, agree the first play and the number it moves, then build it inside your stack. Most teams see it running in their first week.

Is Setter software or an agency?

Neither, quite. Agents run the plays inside your stack every day, and a GTM engineer designs them, checks them and owns the number each one moves. There is no new platform for your team to learn.

Do you replace our RevOps team?

No. We work alongside them. They keep the strategy and the ownership, we build and run the systems, and every change is logged where they can see it.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. Everything runs in your accounts: your CRM, your data tools, your sending domains. If you stop working with us, it all stays with you.

Which tools do you work with?

HubSpot and Salesforce for CRM, plus the data, enrichment, sequencing and product analytics tools you already pay for. If something is missing, we tell you before we add anything.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in your systems. New plays are tested before they touch live records, a person approves anything a buyer sees, and every change is logged.

Where should we start?

With a 30-minute GTM systems review. Most teams start with one part of the engine, usually Find or Hold, and add the rest once the first play is working.
