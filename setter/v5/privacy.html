What this website collects, why, and how to reach us about it.

What we collect

When you book a review, the details you enter (your name, work email, company website and team size) go to our scheduling provider with your booking, and we use them to prepare for the call. If you email us, we keep your message so we can reply.

Booking a time

Times are booked through our scheduling provider. When you open the calendar, your details are passed along so you don't have to type them twice. The provider's own privacy policy covers how they handle it.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and email details for as long as we are talking with you about working together. Ask us and we will delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted at any time.
