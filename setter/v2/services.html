How it works

Point. Hunt. Set. Sharpen.

A setter finds the target, holds the point and lets the hunter take the shot. Every Setter engagement runs the same way.

  1. 01

    Point

    A GTM systems review: your stack, your data, your funnel. We agree on the outcomes we'll own.

  2. 02

    Hunt

    We map your market, model your ideal customer and wire the signals that matter into your CRM.

  3. 03

    Set

    We build the workflows inside your stack, test them, and switch them on one at a time.

  4. 04

    Sharpen

    Agents run it every day. Each week your engineer shows what worked, fixes what didn't and ships the next workflow.
