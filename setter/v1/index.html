What is GTM engineering?

The systems work behind revenue: getting the right data, signals and automation into your CRM so every rep, campaign and play works on the right accounts at the right time.

Is Setter software or an agency?

Neither, quite. We build the workflows inside your stack, run them every day and own the number each one moves. There is no new platform for your team to learn.

Do you replace our RevOps team?

No. We give them a GTM engineering team on tap. They keep the strategy and the ownership. We build and run the systems.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. Everything runs in your accounts: your CRM, your data tools, your sending domains. If you leave, it all stays with you.

Which tools do you work with?

HubSpot and Salesforce for CRM, plus the data, enrichment and sequencing tools you already pay for. If you have none, we set up the stack with you.

Where should we start?

With a GTM systems review. Most teams then start with one service line, usually Data and Signals or Inbound and Routing, and add the rest once it's working.
