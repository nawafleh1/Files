Why Setter

More tools never meant more revenue.

The average B2B SaaS team now runs a CRM, a data provider, an enrichment tool, a sequencer, a routing tool, product analytics and a handful of AI apps. Someone still has to connect all of it, keep the data clean and turn signals into action every day. That someone is usually a founder, a RevOps lead or a sales leader with a dozen other jobs.

AI changed what's possible. Agents can watch every account, enrich every lead, route in seconds and keep a CRM clean. Left alone, they also make confident mistakes at scale.

So Setter pairs them with people. Agents for the work. GTM engineers for the calls. You get a full GTM engineering team, one person accountable for it, and pricing tied to the outcomes it delivers instead of seats.

How we work

Four rules we don't bend.

01

Outcomes over activity

We don't report workflows built or tasks run. We report the number each service line owns.

02

A person signs off

Nothing reaches a buyer or changes your CRM in bulk until your GTM engineer has checked it.

03

Your stack, your data

Everything we build lives in your tools and stays yours. Nothing is locked in our system.

04

Show the work

Every workflow is documented, every run is logged, and your team can see all of it.
