## 1. One page summary

### What the research says, in one paragraph
For every one of your five goals, a behaviour outranks every supplement. Guided CBT with exposure and response prevention is the only thing with large effects on health anxiety and checking (within group g about 2.0, remission 44 to 48%; Axelsson 2019, 2024 cohort). Caffeine control is the second biggest anxiety lever (at or above 400 mg/day, anxiety SMD 2.86 vs 0.61 below it; Liu 2024). Sleep regularity, morning light, a 13:00 caffeine cutoff and finishing hard training 4 hours before bed are what move Whoop Sleep Performance (Windred 2024; Gardiner 2023; Leota 2025). Natural testosterone in a healthy 24 year old is protected by sleep (5 h sleep cuts T 10 to 15%; Leproult 2011), by not over cutting (contest prep deficits cut T 70%; Rossow 2013, Pardue 2017) and by keeping dietary fat at 25 to 35% (low fat diets cut T 10 to 15%; Whittaker 2021). No legal supplement moves T in a healthy young man by more than about 10 to 15%, and the two herbs that do (ashwagandha, tongkat ali) are excluded here for liver and anxiety reasons. Fat loss is calories, protein and lifting; every fat loss supplement is worth under 1 kg over the whole cut. Supplements in this protocol are a 10 to 20% layer on top of the behaviours, chosen because they are safe without labs and carry no detachment, blunting or arousal signal.

### Top 5 non supplement levers (these beat every supplement)

1. Guided CBT with exposure and response prevention for health anxiety and checking, plus metacognitive therapy techniques for rumination. Tier A. Start by Day 7. Internet delivered is non inferior to face to face (Axelsson 2020 JAMA Psychiatry, n 204).
2. Caffeine capped at 200 mg, morning only, nothing after 13:00, always with L-theanine. Tier A. Expect anxiety to drop within 1 to 2 weeks if you are currently above 300 mg.
3. Sleep regularity: wake 06:30 every day, lights out 21:30, 10 to 30 minutes of outdoor light within an hour of waking. Tier A (Windred 2024, n 60,977: regular sleepers had 20 to 48% lower mortality; regularity now counts in Whoop Sleep Performance).
4. Paced breathing at 5.5 to 6 breaths per minute, 15 minutes daily, with a chest strap for feedback. Tier A for anxiety (g 0.81 to 0.83). Physiological sigh for acute spikes (Balban 2023; acute arousal effect replicated, mood superiority not).
5. A 600 kcal deficit with 170 g protein, fat at 65 g, lifting intensity maintained, running capped at 25 km/week easy pace, 10,000 to 12,000 steps. Tier A for fat loss and muscle retention (Helms 2014; ISSN 2026 position stand).

### Honest ceilings
- Anxiety and checking: therapy plus caffeine control can realistically remove most checking and reassurance seeking within 8 to 12 weeks; DP/DR episodes fade as the fear of them fades (Hunter 2003 model; CBT-f-DDD feasibility RCT 2025: 29% no longer met criteria). Supplements here are modest: Silexan SMD 0.35, omega-3 near zero in non clinical samples.
- Focus: there is no limitless pill. Caffeine plus theanine, sleep, exercise and creatine give small, reliable gains in reaction time and attention. Modafinil in rested healthy adults is SMD 0.12 (Roberts 2020). The biggest focus gain for you will come from fixing sleep and anxiety.
- Fat loss: 19% to 12% on the same InBody machine by 31 Dec is achievable but at the top of the safe rate (0.76% bodyweight per week) with zero slack. DXA level 12% likely lands in late January. Expect 0.5 to 1.0 kg true lean loss. See Section 4.
- Testosterone: expect no noticeable change from any supplement here. Expect a mild dip in the last weeks of the cut and recovery within weeks of maintenance. Behaviour protects it.
- Sleep: 100% Whoop Sleep Performance every night is not realistic; 100% on most nights and a weekly average above 90% is. Whoop staging error is plus or minus 15 to 30 minutes per stage, which is larger than most interventions, so judge on weekly averages.

### 2.1 The anxiety and DP/DR behavioural protocol (runs all 90 days)

- Enrol by Day 7 in one guided program: This Way Up "Health Anxiety" course (Australian, accepts international users, therapist guided, Newby 2018 RCT) or NOCD telehealth ERP (checking focus; Feusner 2022 cohort n 3,552, g 1.0). Either works from the US, Dubai or Amman. Budget 2 to 4 hours per week.
- Response prevention list (no exceptions): no symptom googling, no re asking questions you already asked, no asking anyone "do I seem normal", no pulse or body checking, no mirror or hand staring, no "do I feel real" tests, no mid day Whoop or Eight Sleep checks.
- Keep running your "don't answer the alarm" drill. When a DP/DR or health alarm fires: name it ("alarm"), one physiological sigh, return attention to the external task. Do not evaluate whether it worked.
- Grounding for spikes: name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you touch. External anchors only.
- Not allowed during these 90 days: body scan meditation, open monitoring meditation over 10 minutes, retreats, breath holds, Wim Hof or holotropic breathwork, cold plunges. Dissociation and depersonalisation are among the most common meditation adverse events (Farias 2020: 8.3% pooled adverse event rate; Britton 2021: 6 to 14% lasting effects; Pons 2026: over a third of meditation triggered DP/DR began under 100 hours of practice). Cold immersion drives a 2 to 3 fold noradrenaline surge and showed no mood or anxiety benefit in the 2025 meta-analysis (Cain 2025).
- Exercise dose for anxiety is already covered by your training (600 to 1,000 MET minutes per week gives g -0.84 at 12 weeks; 2026 network meta-analysis).

### 2.2 Whoop recovery colour rules for training (HRV guided training: Manresa-Rocamora 2021 meta-analysis, Düking 2021)

- Green (67+): train as programmed.
- Yellow (34 to 66): keep the session, cap at RPE 7, drop the last set of each exercise, run at zone 2 only.
- Red (under 34): 20 to 40 minutes easy walk or mobility. No heavy compounds, no run, no sauna.
- Two consecutive Yellows with the 7 day HRV trend below your baseline: swap the next hard day for an easy day.
- Judge on the 7 day HRV trend, never one night. A 15 to 20 ms single night swing is noise.

## 4. Cut nutrition targets

### 4.1 The arithmetic
- InBody scenario (your stated numbers): fat mass 15.2 kg, fat free mass 64.8 kg. At 12% with FFM unchanged, bodyweight 73.6 kg. Fat to lose 6.4 kg in 10.5 weeks = 0.61 kg/week = 0.76% of bodyweight per week = about 670 kcal/day average deficit. This is the top of the Helms 2014 band (0.5 to 1.0%/week) with no slack.
- InBody underestimates body fat by about 2 to 4 points versus DXA in lean male athletes. If DXA truth is 22%, 12% needs 9.1 kg of fat loss (1.1%/week, 950 kcal/day deficit), which costs muscle and testosterone.
- Resolution: the 31 Dec target is 12% on the same InBody machine that read 17.0% in June, fasted, same time of day, no training in the prior 12 h. Expected scale weight 73 to 74 kg on 31 Dec. Expected true lean loss 0.5 to 1.0 kg. Expected total scale loss 6.5 to 7.5 kg.

- Measured BMR 1,747 kcal. Estimated TDEE with 5 sessions per week plus 10,000 to 12,000 steps: about 2,700 kcal (rest day about 2,450, lift day about 2,750, lift plus run day about 2,950). TDEE drifts down 100 to 150 kcal by week 10.

### 4.4 Built in adjustment rules (these are part of the protocol, not re optimisation)
- Weeks 1 to 2: ignore the scale (creatine water).
- From week 3: if the Sunday 7 day average is falling less than 0.3 kg/week for two consecutive Sundays, add 2,000 steps/day first. If still under 0.3 kg/week the following Sunday, drop training days to 2,150 and rest days to 1,800.
- If the average falls more than 0.9 kg/week for two consecutive Sundays, add 150 kcal of carbs on training days.
- If top set loads on bench, row, overhead press or chin up drop more than 5 to 10% for two weeks, or libido and morning erections disappear with flat mood and poor sleep, insert the diet break early. That cluster is the best free proxy for over dieting and low T.
- Diet break week (24 to 30 Nov) is fixed. ICECAP (Peos 2021, n 61) showed breaks do not speed fat loss but lower hunger and restore training quality; it costs about 0.4 kg of end point.
- 1 Jan 2027: move to maintenance 2,600 kcal immediately to recover T, training quality and sleep. Do not extend the cut.

### 4.5 Training guardrails (your program stays; these are limits)
- Upper body 10 to 16 hard sets per muscle per week; lower body 6 to 10 (running adds leg load).
- Running 20 to 25 km/week, zone 2 only (60 to 70% max HR). HIIT gives no extra fat loss when work is matched (Keating 2017) and costs recovery and sleep.
- Lifting and running on the same day: separate by at least 3 h (Schumann 2022).
- No cold water immersion within 4 h after lifting (blunts hypertrophy; Roberts 2015, Fyfe 2019) and none at all during this protocol for the anxiety reason in Section 2.1.
- Monthly InBody on the same machine: Day 1, Day 29, Day 57, Day 73 (31 Dec), Day 90.

## 5. Sleep protocol (target: 100% Whoop Sleep Performance on most nights)

### 5.1 How Whoop scores it now (2025 rework, Whoop 5.0/MG)
Sleep Performance is a composite of hours slept vs Sleep Need, Sleep Consistency (bed and wake similarity over 4 days, target 90%+), Sleep Efficiency (asleep divided by time in bed, target 90%+) and Sleep Stress (fraction of sleep with high HR or respiratory rate). Weights are unpublished. Sleep Need = baseline plus sleep debt plus strain allowance minus naps (member average 8 h 34 min). The arithmetic that gets you to 100%: time in bed = Sleep Need divided by efficiency. A 8.5 h need at 92% efficiency needs 9 h 15 min in bed. Never let debt stack; two short nights push Need above 10 h, which is unreachable.

### 5.2 Rules, in order of effect
1. Wake 06:30 every day, within 30 minutes. Lights out 21:30. Use the Sleep Planner on "Peak" with a fixed wake time and go to bed 15 minutes before it suggests.
2. Outdoor light 10 to 30 minutes within an hour of waking.
3. Caffeine 200 mg max, none after 13:00 (Gardiner 2023: caffeine cuts total sleep 45 minutes and deep sleep 11 minutes; 8.8 h cutoff for one coffee).
4. Hard training done by 17:30 (Leota 2025: exercise ending 4 h or more before sleep showed no penalty).
5. Sauna ending by 19:50 or warm shower at 20:45 (passive heating 1 to 2 h before bed shortens sleep onset about 10 minutes; Haghayegh 2019, 17 studies).
6. Light under 50 lux from 19:30, phone out of the bedroom at 21:00. Blue blocking glasses are optional and do not replace dimming (Cochrane 2023: probably no difference).
7. Last food 18:30. No alcohol.
8. Bedroom 18 to 20 C air, blackout, contoured eye mask, silence (use steady noise only to mask variable noise).
9. Stimulus control: if awake more than 20 minutes, get up. No clock watching. Never sleep in after a bad night; the fixed wake time is the system.
10. Nap 20 minutes maximum, before 15:30.
11. Supplements at 20:30: magnesium bisglycinate 300 mg, glycine 3 g, L-theanine 200 mg. Melatonin is not nightly; 0.5 to 1 mg only for travel (Section 5.4).
12. No mouth tape (Rhee 2025: 10 poor quality studies, airway risk), no weighted blanket needed (small anxiety effect, SMD 0.40, but heat retention conflicts with bed cooling).

### 5.3 Eight Sleep settings
Evidence basis: one company funded, non randomised study (Chang 2024, Bioengineering, n 54): deep sleep plus 14 minutes in men with a cool early night, HRV plus 7%, HR minus 1.2 bpm, REM trended down in men with aggressive cooling. Physiology (A tier): sleep onset needs a core temperature drop driven by warm extremities; deep sleep concentrates in the first 4 h while core temperature falls; REM in the second half is vulnerable to cold because thermoregulation is suppressed.

- Days 1 to 14: Autopilot on, so it calibrates.
- From Day 15, manual schedule:
  - Bedtime phase (21:30 to about 22:00): plus 1.
  - Deep phase (22:00 to 02:00): minus 3.
  - REM phase (02:00 to 06:00): minus 1.
  - Wake: thermal alarm ramps to plus 3 over the 30 minutes before 06:30, plus vibration.
- Change one phase by one level only at a Sunday review, and only if the 2 week average of deep sleep, wake events or sleep efficiency has worsened. In Dubai run the deep phase one level cooler (minus 4) because ambient and bedding are warmer.
- Base elevation: use the automatic anti snore lift if you have the Base; otherwise flat.

### 5.4 Travel: US to Jordan or Dubai (eastward 7 to 9 h), Tier A protocol (Eastman and Burgess; Janse van Rensburg 2019)
- 3 days before flying: shift bed and wake 30 to 60 minutes earlier each day, 30 minutes bright light on waking, melatonin 0.5 to 1 mg at 17:00 to 18:00 US time, caffeine before 11:00 only.
- Flight: watch on destination time at boarding; sleep during destination night with mask and earplugs; 0.5 to 1 mg melatonin at destination bedtime if awake then.
- Arrival day 1: avoid bright outdoor light before about 12:00 local (sunglasses, indoors), seek light 13:00 to 17:00. Day 2: avoid before 10:30. Day 3: avoid before 09:00. Day 4 onward: light at wake.
- Melatonin 0.5 to 1 mg at local bedtime for 4 to 5 nights (USP Verified, Life Extension 500 mcg or Nature Made; bring it from the US because low doses are rarely stocked). Melatonin is sold in UAE and Jordanian pharmacies but mostly at 3 to 5 mg.
- Local meal times from day 1. Light training only for 2 days, in the afternoon light window. Expect Yellow or Red recoveries and HRV 10 to 20% under baseline for 3 to 5 days; it is not illness.
- Westward return: evening light, avoid early morning light for 2 days, no melatonin unless waking at 03:00 (then 0.5 mg at that waking).

## 8. Skipped list, with reasons

### Excluded for your anxiety and DP/DR profile (would conflict with goal 1)
- Ashwagandha (KSM-66, Shoden, Sensoril): B+ tier for a 10 to 15% T rise in healthy men (Fornalik 2026 meta-analysis, MD plus 57 ng/dL) and B for sleep and anxiety, but LiverTox lists it as a probable hepatotoxin (2026 case series of 26 patients; TGA alert Feb 2024; a 2024 case after five 450 mg capsules), you cannot monitor liver enzymes, and the consumer reports of flat, numb, detached mood overlap exactly with DP/DR. Huberman limits it to one week at a time; Denmark advises against it.
- Tongkat ali: B tier, but the only healthy young men trial (Chan 2021, n 32, 2 weeks) raised estradiol 30% alongside T 15%; meta-analytic effect is significant only in hypogonadal men (Leisegang 2022); reported insomnia and restlessness; two liver injury case reports (2024, 2025). Conflicts with goals 1 and 5.
- Rhodiola, Panax ginseng, L-tyrosine, paraxanthine, nicotine, yohimbine, synephrine: all raise arousal; the "less anxiogenic" paraxanthine claim comes from n 12 to 13 trials by one funded group with anxiety never measured; nicotine attention gains come with dependence and anxious withdrawal; yohimbine works fasted but raises anxiety and blood pressure.
- Kanna (Zembrin): serotonergic, user reports of feeling spaced out, no chronic RCT.
- Bacopa: A tier for memory after 12 weeks (Kongkeaw 2014) but minority reports of fatigue and emotional flattening, plus GI upset. Revisit after the DP/DR episode is 6 months behind you.
- Cold water immersion and cold showers: 2025 meta-analysis (Cain, 11 studies, n 3,177) found no mood or anxiety benefit and a transient inflammation rise; cold shock drives a 2 to 3 fold noradrenaline surge; it also blunts hypertrophy after lifting. Huberman's 11 minutes per week is skipped.
- Body scan meditation, open monitoring, retreats, Wim Hof or holotropic breathwork: dissociation is among the most common meditation adverse events (Farias 2020; Britton 2021; Pons 2026). Hyperventilation styles provoke tingling and derealisation.
- Continuous glucose monitors, daily Whoop checking, Whoop MG ECG for symptoms, daily body fat scales: each is a reassurance seeking tool for you.
- Melatonin nightly at 3 to 10 mg: next day flatness and vivid dreams; meta-analytic onset effect is only 7 minutes (Ferracioli-Oda 2013); Walker advises against it in healthy adults. Kept only at 0.5 to 1 mg for travel.
- 5-HTP, tryptophan: serotonergic, no good sleep RCTs, side effects that read as panic triggers.
- Mouth tape: C tier, airway risk (Rhee 2025).

### Excluded because they need labs or a prescription, or are clinically supervised
- DHEA (prohormone, raises estradiol, needs DHEA-S and estradiol), kava (liver monitoring), green tea extract capsules (EFSA 2018: transaminase rises at or above 800 mg EGCG, worse fasted; brewed tea is fine), berberine and dihydroberberine (CYP3A4 and 2D6 inhibition, glucose lowering that mimics anxiety symptoms, no effect in lean people), high dose vitamin D above 4,000 IU, zinc above 15 mg daily (copper deficiency), high dose B6, lithium orotate, iodine beyond RDA.
- Every Blueprint prescription and procedure (Section 6).
- SARMs, anabolic steroids, TRT, peptides including BPC-157: excluded by rule and by the "natural" goal.

### Excluded as C tier (no adequate human evidence for your goals)
- Fadogia agrestis: zero human trials; rat testicular toxicity at all doses in the 28 day study (Yakubu 2008).
- Boron: one 8 man, 1 week, uncontrolled study (Naghii 2011), never replicated; an earlier Naghii 1997 study found T unchanged. Covered at RDA level in the multivitamin anyway.
- Vitamin K2 for testosterone: rat data only (Ito 2011). RDA level K2 is in the multivitamin for bone.
- Standalone zinc for testosterone: raises T only in deficient men (Te 2023); a 170 g animal protein diet is not zinc deficient.
- D-aspartic acid: 6 g lowered T in trained men (Melville 2015). Tribulus: A tier null. Maca and fenugreek: libido, not T. Shilajit: one RCT in 45 to 55 year olds. Creatine for DHT: one unreplicated 2009 study. Proprietary "test booster" blends: 90% had no human evidence in a 2020 analysis.
- NR, NMN, spermidine, fisetin, calcium alpha-ketoglutarate, taurine, urolithin A (two primary endpoints missed in the largest trials, one small young men study, 125 USD/month), GlyNAC (all efficacy data from one lab in older adults; glycine is kept on its own), astaxanthin, lutein and zeaxanthin for cognition, cocoa flavanols for cognition (COSMOS-Mind null), sulforaphane and hydroxytyrosol for mood, curcumin (heterogeneous small trials; piperine formulations interact with CYP3A4), apigenin isolate (no human trial), oral GABA, valerian (null on objective sleep), chamomile (HAMA MD -1.8, mild), tart cherry (2026 meta-analysis null for sleep), lemon balm, inositol megadoses (2014 meta-analysis null, GI), NAC (OCD augmentation only, transient, no rumination data), lion's mane (2025 acute trial null with some tasks worse), ginkgo, phosphatidylserine, racetams, alpha-GPC (stroke HR 1.43 in a 10 year Korean cohort, dose responsive; citicoline is the safe choice), HMB, phosphatidic acid, betaine, epicatechin, CLA (trivial, worsens insulin sensitivity), L-carnitine (minus 1.2 kg only in overweight people, TMAO concern), capsaicin capsules (cook with chili), ketone esters (cost, GI, unexplained anxiety signal in a 2026 runner trial), sodium bicarbonate (wrong event type), beetroot nitrate (A for recreational endurance but blunted in trained athletes; harmless if you like it before a run).
- Devices: red and near infrared light (no human RCT for sleep, cognition or testosterone), transcranial photobiomodulation (2025 Vielight funded sham RCT null), Pulsetto, Nurosym and other consumer vagus stimulators (no sham controlled anxiety trial), Apollo Neuro, Sensate, grounding mats, PEMF, binaural beats for daily anxiety, 40 Hz stimulation, blue blocking glasses as a primary tool (Cochrane 2023), Oura in addition to Whoop (redundant), cranial electrotherapy (Alpha-Stim: B for anxiety but the largest independent sham trial was null; only if therapy and breathing fail, under a clinician).
- Training and diet fads: time restricted eating (no advantage once calories match; lowered T in trained men), HIIT for fat loss (no advantage when work matched), sprint intervals during the cut (fine in January), blood flow restriction (useful tool, not needed with a home gym), sauna as a fat loss tool (100 to 150 kcal per session), cold for fat loss (20 to 60 kcal).

### Discuss with a doctor only (never self administered; listed because you asked for honesty)
- SSRIs (sertraline or escitalopram first line in practice; fluoxetine and paroxetine beat placebo in health anxiety RCTs, Fallon 2017 and Greeven 2007). Onset 4 to 8 weeks; week 1 to 2 activation can transiently worsen anxiety and rarely DP/DR; emotional blunting occurs in a meaningful minority, which is why therapy comes first for you. Flag saffron if one is started.
- Clomipramine for OCD type checking (Y-BOCS -4.72 vs placebo, not clearly better than SSRIs; needs ECG).
- Lamotrigine for DP/DR: inconsistent evidence (Somer 2013 review), rash risk.
- Modafinil or methylphenidate: SMD 0.12 in rested healthy adults (Roberts 2020), raise arousal, controlled in the UAE and Jordan. Only if ADHD is actually suspected.
- Trazodone (Attia's sleep stack) and GLP-1 drugs (you do not meet criteria, and they shed lean mass): not for you.
