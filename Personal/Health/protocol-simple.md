# The simple version: anxiety first

Day 1 = Tue 20 Oct 2026. Day 90 = Sun 17 Jan 2027. Full detail and evidence in protocol.md. This page is what you actually do.

## The rule that sits above everything
If anxiety rises for two weeks in a row, the first thing you drop is the calorie deficit (go to 2,600 kcal), not the therapy, not the sleep, not the breathing. Hunger and short sleep raise anxiety; nothing in the stack can out-supplement them.

## The 5 things that do the work (in order of effect)

1. Therapy, started by Day 7. One guided program: This Way Up "Health Anxiety" (online, therapist guided) or NOCD (ERP for checking). 2 to 4 hours per week, 3 to 4 evenings at 19:00. This is the only thing with a large effect on checking and health anxiety.
2. Caffeine: 100 mg at 08:00, optional 100 mg at 09:45 on lifting days, hard cap 200 mg, nothing after 13:00, always with L-theanine. No pre workouts, no energy drinks. If you are above 300 mg today, taper over one week.
3. Sleep: wake 06:30 every day, lights out 21:30, 10 to 30 minutes outdoors within an hour of waking. Hard training done by 17:30. No food after 18:30. Lights low from 19:30. Phone out of the bedroom at 21:00.
4. Breathing: 15 minutes at 6 breaths per minute (4.5 s in, 5.5 s out) at 07:15 with a Polar H10 and the Elite HRV app. For a spike during the day: one physiological sigh (two inhales through the nose, one long exhale through the mouth), then back to the task.
5. Training as programmed, 5 days, plus 10,000 to 12,000 steps. Running easy only, 20 to 25 km/week. No HIIT, no cold plunges, no sauna on red recovery days.

## Response prevention list (no exceptions, all 90 days)
No symptom googling. No re asking a question you already asked. No asking anyone if you seem normal. No pulse or body checking. No "do I feel real" tests. No Whoop, Eight Sleep or scale checking except Sunday 08:00. Whoop notifications off. No body scan meditation, no retreats, no breath holds, no Wim Hof.

When the alarm fires: name it ("alarm"), one sigh, return to what you were doing. Do not check whether it worked. Health worries get written down at 18:00 in a 15 minute window and the notebook closes.

## The stack, simplified

Morning (07:35 with breakfast): Silexan lavender 80 mg (from Day 8), multivitamin 2 caps (from Day 29), creatine 5 g (Day 1).
08:00: coffee 100 mg plus L-theanine 200 mg (Day 1). Citicoline 250 mg (from Day 43).
12:00 lunch: omega-3 4 softgels, about 2 g EPA plus DHA (from Day 22). Ashwagandha KSM-66 300 mg (Day 15 to Day 71).
20:30: magnesium bisglycinate 300 mg, glycine 3 g, L-theanine 200 mg (Day 1 and Day 36), ashwagandha 300 mg (Day 15 to 71), saffron 28 mg (from Day 57), psychobiotic (from Day 71).
Run days: beetroot shot 2 to 3 h before the run.
Protein: whey isolate to reach 170 g per day.

Add dates, one at a time: Day 1 creatine, theanine, magnesium. Day 8 Silexan. Day 15 ashwagandha. Day 22 omega-3. Day 29 multivitamin. Day 36 glycine (and decide Silexan 80 or 160 mg). Day 43 citicoline. Day 50 creatine to 10 g. Day 57 saffron (14 mg for a week, then 28 mg). Day 71 ashwagandha ends, psychobiotic starts.

Not in the stack, decided: tongkat ali, fadogia, boron, melatonin nightly, CBD, kava, anything stimulant.

## Food (keep it boring)
Training days 2,300 kcal: protein 170 g, fat 65 g, carbs 260 g. Rest days 1,900 kcal: protein 175 g, fat 70 g, carbs 140 g. Four meals of about 40 to 45 g protein (07:35, 12:00, 15:30, 18:30), slow protein at dinner. Diet break week 24 to 30 Nov at 2,700 kcal. Maintenance 2,600 kcal from 1 Jan. Water 3 L, salt your food.

## Sleep settings
Eight Sleep: Autopilot for 2 weeks, then bedtime plus 1, deep phase minus 3, late night minus 1, thermal alarm to plus 3 over the last 30 minutes. Room 18 to 20 C. Warm 10 minute shower at 20:45 on non sauna nights. Sauna 15 to 20 minutes ending by 19:50, 3 to 4 nights per week.

## Sunday 08:00, 10 minutes, the only data you look at
1. 7 day average weight (from week 3: down 0.5 to 0.7 kg per week; if not, add 2,000 steps first).
2. 7 day average Whoop Sleep Performance (target 90%+; under 85% two Sundays running means lights out 15 minutes earlier).
3. Checking count for the week (should fall; if it rises two Sundays running, book an extra therapy session).

## Stop rules
Dark urine, yellow eyes or skin, itching, right upper belly pain, unexplained nausea: stop ashwagandha, Silexan and saffron, see a doctor. Emotional flatness after adding an item: stop that item only. Rash: stop the last item added. Nothing else changes until 17 Jan 2027.

## If it is not enough by Day 60
That is the point to see a doctor about an SSRI (sertraline or escitalopram) alongside the therapy, not before. Tell them about saffron and ashwagandha.
